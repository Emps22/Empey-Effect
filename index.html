Our mission

Spread positivity. Uplift hearts. Remind people they're not alone.

You don't know what someone is going through today. A simple "I hope you're having a good day" can change it.

Whether you're soaring or just barely holding it together, this space is here to offer encouragement, kindness, and a sense of community. With every quote, story, and shared moment, we hope to spark change — starting with you.

This isn't a religious page, and it isn't for profit. It's simply somewhere people can go where someone cares enough to offer a little light in a dark world.

Why I started this

I'm a safety professional — it's my job and my passion to watch out for people. At a safety conference in 2025, a presentation on positivity stuck with me: you never know what the person next to you is carrying. One kind word, one moment of attention, can genuinely make someone's day.

Rooted in Waterloo, open to everyone

The Empey Effect started as a Facebook page for my own community — tired of the negativity flooding the news and social media. It's grown into something bigger, and everyone is welcome here, wherever you're logging in from.

Small acts, real effect

Kindness doesn't need a plan

It just needs a moment. Here's where to start — offline and on.

In real life

  • Smile or say helloSimple, human, and often overlooked — a warm greeting can go a long way.
  • Hold the door openA small gesture that shows respect and presence.
  • Give a genuine compliment"Your energy is great," or "that color looks amazing on you."
  • Leave a kind note in publicA sticky note on a mirror, a bench, or a vending machine.
  • Help carry something heavyLend a hand when you see someone struggling.
  • Pick up litterQuiet kindness to the earth and your community.
  • Let someone go ahead of youEspecially if they have fewer items or seem in a rush.
  • Say thank you, and mean itTo cashiers, janitors, drivers, delivery workers — with eye contact.

In the digital world

  • Leave encouraging commentsEspecially on posts that might otherwise go unnoticed.
  • Share a small business or artistBoosting someone's visibility is a real kindness.
  • Choose empathy over argumentsTake the high road, and offer calm instead.
  • Tag a friend in something upliftingSpread joy, not just memes and reels.
